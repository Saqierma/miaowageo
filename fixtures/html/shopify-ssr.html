Acme Hydraulic Cylinders

Acme manufactures double-acting hydraulic cylinders for agricultural and construction machinery. Bore sizes range from 40 mm to 200 mm, with stroke lengths up to 3000 mm. All units are tested to 1.5 times rated working pressure before shipment.

Certifications: ISO 9001:2015, CE marking for the European market, and SGS factory inspection reports available on request. Minimum order quantity is 100 pieces for standard models and 20 pieces for custom bore configurations.

Lead time is 25 to 35 days after deposit for standard models. Sea freight from Ningbo port. FOB and CIF terms both available.
